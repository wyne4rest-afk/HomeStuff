## What's in a Real Car Shampoo?

A good shampoo is a **pH-neutral blend of surfactants with a bit of lubricant**. Here's a starter formula if you'd like to make your own.

## Recipe

**Yield:** about 500 g of concentrate

| Ingredient | Amount | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Distilled water | ~327 g (about 65%) | Base |
| Sodium C14-16 olefin sulfonate (~40%) or SLES | 75 g (15%) | Main cleaning surfactant |
| Cocamidopropyl betaine (~30%) | 50 g (10%) | Foam booster, mildness |
| Decyl glucoside (~50%) | 25 g (5%) | Gentle cleaner |
| Glycerin | 15 g (3%) | Slickness |
| Preservative (e.g., a phenoxyethanol-based one such as Liquid Germall Plus) | 3-5 g (0.5-1%) | Prevents bacteria and mold |
| Sodium gluconate or EDTA *(optional)* | 1 g (0.2%) | Helps with hard water |
| Salt | 1-3 g | Thickens |
| Citric acid solution | A few drops | Adjusts pH |
| Fragrance and dye *(optional)* | A few drops | Cosmetic |

All of these are available from soap-making and cosmetic ingredient suppliers, or online.

---

## Equipment

- [ ] Digital scale (0.1 g accuracy)
- [ ] Glass or plastic beakers and a stirring rod or spoon
- [ ] pH test strips (a 5-9 range works well)
- [ ] Gloves and goggles
- [ ] Clean bottles for storage

> **Safety:** Concentrated surfactants irritate skin and eyes. Wear gloves and goggles while mixing.

---

## Process

1. **Weigh** the distilled water into a beaker.
2. **Add the surfactants** one at a time, stirring slowly to avoid excess foam.
3. **Mix in** the glycerin and the chelating agent, if using.
4. **Add the preservative**, then fragrance and dye if desired.
5. **Check the pH.** You're aiming for about 7 to 8. Add citric acid solution drop by drop to lower it.
6. **Thicken with salt**, a pinch at a time, until the mixture thickens slightly. Too much will make it thin out again.
7. **Rest it** for a day so the bubbles settle, then bottle it.

---

## How to Use It

| Dilution | Use |
|---|---|
| 15-30 ml per 4 L of water | Standard wash |
| A little more | Very dirty vehicle |

---

## Caveats

- **Less slick than commercial products.** Commercial shampoos usually contain proprietary polymer lubricants, so a DIY batch may be less slippery. Compensate with plenty of suds and a clean wash mitt.
- **Don't skip the preservative.** Without one, a water-based mix will grow mold within days.
- **Test small first.** Make small batches so you can adjust thickness and foam to your liking.

## Motorcycle Tips

- Avoid spraying high-pressure water into bearings, the chain, and electrical connectors.
- Keep soap off the brake discs.

---

## Bottom Line

A bottle of decent pH-neutral car shampoo is inexpensive and probably less hassle than making your own. But if you want to experiment, this formula will get you a working batch.
